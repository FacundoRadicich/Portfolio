Which dormant accounts should sales call first?

Registered accounts with zero orders, scored 0–100. Move the weights to see the ranking change.

ScoreAccountCategoryRegionTier

Method: tier maps Strategic/Key/Potential/Standard to 100/85/70/40. Category fit and regional strength are each category's and region's share of active buyers, scaled so the strongest equals 100. Weights are normalized to sum to 100%. Showing the top 15 of accounts.

Production stack: Python · Odoo XML-RPC · pandas · HTML/JS. This demo generates its data in the browser with a fixed seed, so it looks the same on every visit.
